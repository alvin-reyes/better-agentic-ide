Sub-terminals

Mockup for 2026-11-02-sub-terminals-ux.md. Terminals opened with ade open join the caller's tab as sub-terminals in a strip under the tab bar. Click entries to switch, hover for ×, right-click an entry or a main tab for its menu, focus the strip and use ← → Home End Enter Delete, and use the scenarios on the right. Keys in this page: [ ] stand in for ⌘[ ⌘], k for ⌘K (the browser owns the real ones).
