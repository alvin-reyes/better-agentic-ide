Saved servers (SSH), slice S1: UX mockup

A stand-in Better Terminal window with the tab bar, terminal panes, the command palette and the server list. Spec: 2026-11-18-saved-servers-ux.md, beside this file. Everything inside the framed window is the design; the dashed boxes above and to the right are demo controls. Nothing here connects to anything: the "start-up files" control decides what a Connect pretends to see. Host, user and client names are invented. The 30 s hand-over bound runs as 3 s while “Fast timer” is ticked. Keys: Ctrl+P or ⌘P opens the palette, Ctrl+K or ⌘K the tab switcher, Ctrl or ⌘ with ← and → moves between panes, Shift+F10 on a tab its menu.
