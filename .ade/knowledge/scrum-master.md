- 2026-10-15: The phone-remote epics keep their stories as `[[entry]]` rows in `tickets.toml` with no pulled files; the full acceptance criteria go inline in `description` and `verify` (TOML literal strings `'''`), because a `bmad-build-auto` run gets only the entry. `tickets.py status` on the epic is the check.
- 2026-11-02: Sub-terminal stories: an entry's `unknown` holds it blocked in `next` until cleared, which is how to mark a story waiting on outside work (not on main); `after` cannot name a branch. `refine = true` keeps a later story out of ready_to_start until it is pulled and refined.
- 2026-11-04: A ticket's open owner questions go in `unknown` (it stays blocked in `next` until cleared) and its full criteria go in `description`. `refine` is cleared by deleting the key, not by setting it false. To replace one entry, splice the text from its `[[entry]]` to the next one, then re-parse the whole file with tomllib.
