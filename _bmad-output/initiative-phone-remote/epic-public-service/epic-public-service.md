# A public free service

## Description

Accounts (Sign in with Apple), abuse controls, privacy policy and App Store review (piece 4). Needs its own spec.

## Outcome

Any ADE user can install the app from the App Store and use the hosted relay for free.

## Done when

1. Sign in with Apple ties devices to an account.
2. Abuse controls bound what one account or address can cost the relay.
3. A privacy policy and App Store review are done; the app is live.
